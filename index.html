Welcome to New Life Fellowship Church

We welcome you to our New Life Fellowship web page. On behalf of our Pastor, Bishop Paul and Lady Sheri Riley, we pray that this website is a blessing to you. Here at New Life Fellowship, we preach and live the Word of God. In the book of John 14:6 it tells us that “no one will come to the father except through Jesus Christ”. We believe in the scriptures and in the Holy Spirit, we believe that Jesus Christ is our Lord and saviour. Our congregation is a family that works together as one in the body of Christ. We offer the options to watch the services live on our Facebook page, we stream every Sunday and Tuesday night and you even have the opportunity to go back and re-watch videos of your choice. May the Lord bless you and keep you as our prayer.
